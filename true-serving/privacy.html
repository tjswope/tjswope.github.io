Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Introduction

True Serving ("we," "our," or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. The App doesn't require an account, and we don't run servers that store your data.

Data Stored on Your Device

The meals you log, including their photos, foods and nutrition, your goals, your food history and your settings, are stored only on your iPhone. We don't receive a copy. Deleting the App deletes this data.

AI Features and Google Gemini

Photo estimates, LiDAR portion scans, the Coach chat and the Insights overview use Google's Gemini AI, accessed through Google Firebase. Before anything is sent, the App explains what will be shared and asks for your permission. Depending on the feature you use, the following is sent to Google:

  • Photo estimates: the meal photos you take, measurements from a 3D (LiDAR) scan, and any description you type
  • Packaged food portions: the product's name and serving size, with the photo and measurements from a LiDAR scan
  • Insights overview: a summary of your logged meals and goals for the period you're viewing
  • Coach: your messages, with your goals, today's meals, recent averages and foods you log often. For restaurant questions, Gemini may run Google searches based on your message.

Data is sent only when you use one of these features, and only to produce that result. Your name and Apple Account aren't sent. Google processes this data under the Gemini API Additional Terms and the Google Privacy Policy. You can withdraw permission anytime in the App under Account → AI. If you don't allow it, meal photos are analyzed on your device instead, and Coach and the AI overview are turned off.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, the App reads your weight, steps, active and resting energy, exercise minutes and workouts to show your progress. The App never writes to Apple Health. Health data is only included in AI requests (the Insights overview and Coach) if you turn on "Include Apple Health data," which is off unless you choose it. Health data is never used for advertising or sold.

Barcode Lookups

When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a public food database, to look up the product's nutrition. No other information is sent with it.

App Security and Diagnostics

To make sure AI requests come from the genuine App, Firebase App Check uses Apple's App Attest to verify your device. Firebase may process limited technical information, such as the App version, device model and iOS version, to operate this service. The App also keeps a performance log of AI requests on your device; it isn't sent anywhere unless you choose to share it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the free week are handled entirely by Apple. We don't receive or store your payment information.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not use analytics or advertising services
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites
  • We do not access your location
  • We do not sell your data

Deleting Your Data

Delete a meal in the App to remove it, or delete the App to remove all of your data from your device. Data sent to Google for an AI feature is handled under Google's terms above.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date above.

Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact us at: privacy@newwaveapps.com
