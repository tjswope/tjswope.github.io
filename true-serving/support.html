Need help with True Serving? Check out the FAQs below or contact us directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's free, and what needs Plus?

Logging meals by hand, food search, barcode scanning, your meal history, goals, the progress calendar, Apple Health and all charts are free. True Serving Plus adds photo calorie estimates, LiDAR portion scans, the Coach chat and the AI-written Insights overview.

How does the free week work?

Tap "Start Your Free Week" on the Plus screen. It's a free, one-time purchase, not a subscription, so there's nothing to cancel and you're never charged. After seven days the AI features lock until you choose a monthly or yearly plan. The free week is available once per Apple Account.

How do photo estimates work?

Take a photo of your meal and the App identifies each food with its calories, protein, carbs and fat. Adding a few words, like "with ranch dressing," makes the estimate more accurate. You can change any food or amount before saving.

Which iPhones support LiDAR portion scans?

LiDAR is on iPhone Pro and Pro Max models from iPhone 12 Pro onward. On those phones, the camera also measures how much food is on the plate. On other iPhones, photo estimates still work using the photo alone.

Why does the App ask to share data with Google Gemini?

The AI features run on Google's Gemini AI, so your meal photos or meal summaries have to be sent to Google to get a result. The App asks first and explains what's shared. Apple Health data is only included if you turn it on. You can change this anytime in Account → AI. See our Privacy Policy for details.

I reached a daily limit. When does it reset?

Plus includes generous daily limits (and monthly limits for some features) to keep AI costs fair. Daily limits reset at midnight, and monthly limits reset on the 1st. The App shows when each one resets, and you can always log a meal by hand.

How accurate are the calorie estimates?

Estimates are a starting point, not an exact measurement. LiDAR scans make portion sizes more accurate, but sauces, oils and hidden ingredients are hard to see in a photo. Check the foods and amounts on the review screen before you save.

How do I connect Apple Health?

Go to Account → Apple Health → Connect Apple Health. To change what the App can read later, open the Health app, tap your profile, then Apps → True Serving.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the App, go to Account → Manage Subscription. Or open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions → True Serving Plus. You keep Plus until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Open Account and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

Are my meals backed up?

Your meals are stored on your iPhone and included in your regular iPhone backup. If you delete the App, its data is deleted too.
