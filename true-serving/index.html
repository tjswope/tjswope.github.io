Features

What you can do

📸

Snap Your Meal

Take a photo and get each food identified with calories, protein, carbs and fat. Adjust anything before you save.

📐

LiDAR Portions

On iPhone Pro models, a 3D scan measures how much food is actually on the plate, so portions aren't a guess.

🥄

Real-World Amounts

Amounts are shown the way you'd measure them: cups, tablespoons, ounces and slices, not grams.

💬

Ask the Coach

"What should I order?" Coach knows your goals and today's meals, and looks up official restaurant nutrition with sources.

🏷️

Barcodes & History

Scan packaged foods, search foods, or re-log favorites from your history, organized by breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks.

📈

See Your Progress

A calendar of every day, your weight trend, and calories in vs. out with Apple Health, plus an AI-written overview.
