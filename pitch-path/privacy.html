Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

Introduction

Pitch Path ("we," "our," or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App stores and how it is used.

Information We Store

The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Your current level, high score, and daily progress history
  • Game settings (instrument, game mode)
  • Purchase status for the premium unlock

This data is stored on your device using standard iOS storage. It is not transmitted to any server.

Purchase Information

If you purchase Pitch Path Premium, the App stores a record of your purchase status locally using Apple's StoreKit framework. We do not receive or store your payment information — all transactions are handled entirely by Apple.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any account information
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services
  • We do not access your microphone or camera
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not share any data with third parties

Audio

The App generates audio (instrument tones and chords) entirely on your device using synthesis. No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date above.

Contact Us
