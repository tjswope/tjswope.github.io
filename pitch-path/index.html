Features

What you can do

🎸

Guitar + Piano

Practice on a realistic guitar fretboard or a two-octave piano keyboard. Switch between them anytime.

🎵

Interval Training

Hear two notes, then find them. Start with 3rds and 5ths, unlock every interval, then advance to melodies.

👂

Pitch Recognition

Identify individual notes by ear. Each level adds one more note with chord-based calibration to train your memory.

📈

21 Levels

14 interval training levels plus 7 pitch recognition levels. Progressive difficulty from beginner to advanced.

🎯

Learn From Mistakes

Every level starts with calibration. Get an answer wrong and the correct answer plays back so you learn as you go.

📅

Track Progress

Daily progress calendar shows your scores and levels. Unlock levels permanently and pick up where you left off.
