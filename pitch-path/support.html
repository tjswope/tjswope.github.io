Frequently Asked Questions

How does interval training work?

The app plays two notes: a root note and a scale degree. First tap the root note on the fretboard or keyboard, then tap the interval you heard. The app will tell you if you're correct and show the right answer if you're not.

What is pitch recognition mode?

A single note is played and you identify it by tapping the correct key. During calibration, each note's major chord plays first to help you learn its unique sound. Levels start with just C and D and build up through all natural notes.

What is calibration?

At the start of each level, the app plays through all the notes you'll be tested on. Listen carefully, then tap them when prompted. If you're already comfortable with a level, tap Skip to jump straight to gameplay.

How does scoring work?

Each correct answer earns points based on the level and challenge type. Complete an entire level (10 correct in a row) without a mistake to earn a PERFECT bonus worth 10 times the level number. You have 3 lives per game.

Can I replay the notes?

Yes. Tap the replay button in the bottom-right corner to hear the current challenge again. This also resets any taps you've made so you can start fresh.

What's included for free?

The first 3 levels of both interval training and pitch recognition are completely free. A one-time purchase of $2.99 unlocks all 21 levels.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open the menu, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchase." Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
