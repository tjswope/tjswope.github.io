Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2025

Introduction

Rep Repeat ("we," "our," or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal data:

  • Timer Configurations: Names, durations, colors, and settings for timers you create. This is stored locally on your device only.
  • Preferences: Your voice selection and app settings, stored locally.

What We Don't Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics
  • Any data transmitted to external servers

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using Core Data. We do not have servers that receive or store your information.

Apple Watch

If you use the Apple Watch app, timer data is synced between your iPhone and Watch using Apple's built-in Watch Connectivity framework. This data never leaves your devices.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple StoreKit: Used for in-app purchases. Apple handles all payment processing.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information and is suitable for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact us at: privacy@newwaveapps.com
