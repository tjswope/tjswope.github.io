Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new timer?

Tap the + button on the main screen. Enter a name, set the duration, number of repetitions, and optional rest time between reps. Choose a color and save.

How do I change the voice?

Go to Settings and tap "Voice" to choose from available system voices for audio cues.

Can I use it on Apple Watch?

Yes! Install the Watch app from the Watch app on your iPhone. Your timers will sync automatically.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchase". Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

The timer stops when my screen locks. What can I do?

The timer continues running in the background and will play audio cues. Make sure notifications are enabled for the app.
