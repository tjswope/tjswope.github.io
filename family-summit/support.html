Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. Goals, habits, and the daily challenge all work for free with your data stored on your device. You only need an account if you want cloud backup or to share progress with family.

What's the difference between a goal and a task?

A goal is the outcome you're working toward (for example, "Run a 5K by August 1"). Tasks are the recurring habits that get you there (like "Run 3 times a week"). Each goal can have several tasks.

How does the AI coach work?

As you type a goal, an on-device coach (powered by Apple Intelligence) suggests how to make it clearer and more measurable. It runs entirely on your device — your text is never sent anywhere. The coach appears only on devices that support Apple Intelligence.

How do I share with my family?

Turn on Cloud & Family Sharing in the Account tab, then tap "Invite a Family Member" to generate a code. When someone enters your code, you'll get a request to approve before they join. A family can have up to 10 members.

What does the in-app purchase include?

"Family Summit Cloud" is a one-time $4.99 purchase that backs up your goals across devices and unlocks family sharing for up to 10 members. There's no subscription, and you can try it free for 7 days.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open the Account tab, tap Purchase, then "Restore Purchases." Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I delete my account?

Go to the Account tab and tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes your account and all your cloud data. Goals stored only on your device are removed when you delete the app.
