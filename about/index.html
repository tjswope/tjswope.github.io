About

One new app. Every month.

I'm Tom Swope, and I founded New Wave Apps in 2026 with a simple challenge: design, build and ship a brand-new app every month of the year.

By day, I'm a high school computer science teacher, challenging students to solve real problems and bring their own ideas to life with code. I teach AP Computer Science Principles, AP Computer Science A, Honors Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, and Honors Computer Science Seminar where students design and build their own iOS apps.

Each app grows out of an area I genuinely care about: health and fitness, music, personal growth and family life. I build things I want to use myself, and I hope you'll find them useful too.
