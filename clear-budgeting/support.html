Support

Need help with Clear Budgeting? Check out the FAQs below or contact us directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up family sharing?

You need a Pro subscription to use family sharing. Go to Settings > Family and tap "Create Family Group". You can then invite up to 4 additional members by email.

How does receipt scanning work?

Tap the camera icon when adding a transaction, take a photo of your receipt, and the app will automatically extract the store name, amount, and date. You can edit the extracted information before saving.

Can I export my data?

Yes! Go to Settings > Export Data to download your transactions as a CSV file that you can open in Excel or other spreadsheet applications.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to Settings on your iPhone > Your Name > Subscriptions, find Clear Budgeting, and tap Cancel Subscription.

What happens to my data if I cancel Pro?

Your local data remains on your device. However, cloud sync will stop and family members will lose access to shared data. You can re-subscribe at any time to restore these features.

How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

Go to Settings > Subscription and tap "Restore Purchase". Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
