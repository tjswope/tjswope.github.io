Frequently Asked Questions

How do I share a setlist with my band?

Open the setlist, tap the share icon in the top right, and send the .setlist file via AirDrop, Messages, or email. Your band members just tap the file on their device to import it — all songs and settings come with it.

Does my Bluetooth page turner work with this app?

Yes. Most Bluetooth foot pedals and page turners send arrow key or page up/down events, which Worship Metronome uses to navigate between songs. Pair your pedal via iOS Bluetooth settings and it should work immediately.

The metronome stops when I switch apps. How do I fix this?

Make sure your iPhone is not in Silent Mode (check the mute switch on the side). Background audio requires the ringer to be on or headphones connected. Also confirm that Background App Refresh is enabled for Worship Metronome in iOS Settings.

How does the count-in work?

Each song has three count-in options: None (starts immediately), Auto (plays one silent measure then speaks one measure before starting), or Button (you tap "Count In" to trigger a spoken measure on your cue). Set it per song in the song editor.

How do I set up the PDF sheet music on iPad?

In a setlist, tap the document icon to attach a PDF. When you're in landscape orientation on iPad, the PDF opens alongside the metronome in a split view. The page advances automatically when you move to the next song.

How do I add a rest or transition between songs?

In the setlist editor, tap the + button and choose "Add Rest." Give it a label like "Offering" or "Tune Instruments." It appears in the setlist as a non-playing item — just navigate past it when you're ready for the next song.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Go to Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
