Features

What you can do

🎵

Set Lists

Build a setlist for your service with each song's tempo, time signature, and click settings saved and ready to go.

📤

Share With Your Band

Export your setlist and send it to your whole team via AirDrop or email. Charts and album art come along, and they import it with one tap.

🎙️

Count-In

A voice speaks the beats before each song — 1, 2, 3, 4 — so your whole band starts together every time.

🦶

Bluetooth Page Turner

Use a Bluetooth foot pedal to move between songs hands-free while you're at the keys or guitar.

📅

Planning Center Import PRO

Turn Sunday's plan into a setlist in seconds, with each song's tempo, time signature, key, and chord chart.

📄

A Chart for Every Song PRO

Attach a PDF chart to each song and see it next to the click: side by side on iPad, or above the chart in portrait on iPhone and iPad.

🖼️

Album Art Lookup PRO

Search for any song to fill in its title, artist, and album art, so every song in your set is easy to spot.

🔇

Background Audio

Switch to another app and the click keeps running. The tempo never stops between songs or during transitions.

Pro features and unlimited setlists and songs come with Worship Metronome Unlimited, a one-time purchase with no subscription. Try everything free for 14 days.
