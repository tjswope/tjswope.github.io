Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Introduction

Worship Metronome ("we," "our," or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App stores and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Song and Setlist Data

The App stores the songs and setlists you create, including:

  • Song names, artists, keys, tempos, time signatures, and click settings
  • Setlist order and rest period labels
  • PDF charts and album art you attach to songs or setlists
  • Count-in preferences and sound selections

This data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate any server, and none of it is sent to us.

Planning Center (Optional)

If you choose to connect your Planning Center account, you sign in directly with Planning Center; the App never sees your password. The App then reads, from your account:

  • Your service types and plans
  • The items in a plan you choose to import, including song titles, authors, tempos, time signatures, and keys
  • PDF attachments on those songs, if you choose to download charts

Imported information is saved on your device only. The App stores the sign-in token Planning Center issues in your device's secure Keychain so you stay connected. Tapping Disconnect in the App's Settings removes it from your device; you can also revoke the App's access at any time from your Planning Center account. Planning Center's handling of your data is governed by Planning Center's Privacy Policy.

Song Search and Album Art (Optional)

When you look up a song, or import songs with album art turned on, the App sends the song title and artist to Apple's iTunes Search service to find matching songs and album art. Chosen artwork is downloaded and stored on your device. These requests are handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. No account or personal information is included in them.

Purchase Information

If you purchase Worship Metronome Unlimited, or start the free trial, the App checks your purchase status, including when your trial began, using Apple's StoreKit framework. We do not receive or store your payment information — all transactions are handled entirely by Apple.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any account information
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services
  • We do not access your microphone or camera
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers
  • The App contacts Planning Center and Apple only when you use the features described above

Data Storage

  • Local Storage: All songs and setlists are saved on your device
  • Setlist Sharing: When you export a setlist, the file is created on your device and shared only through the method you choose (AirDrop, Messages, email, etc.). The file includes the setlist's songs along with any attached PDF charts and album art. We have no involvement in or access to that transfer

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date above.

Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact us using our contact form.
