Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2025

Introduction

Worship Metronome ("we," "our," or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App stores and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Song and Setlist Data

The App stores the songs and setlists you create, including:

  • Song names, tempos, time signatures, and click settings
  • Setlist order, rest period labels, and attached PDF file references
  • Count-in preferences and sound selections

This data is stored locally on your device. It is not transmitted to any server.

Purchase Information

If you purchase Worship Metronome Unlimited, the App stores a record of your purchase status locally using Apple's StoreKit framework. We do not receive or store your payment information — all transactions are handled entirely by Apple.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any account information
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services
  • We do not access your microphone or camera
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not share any data with third parties

Data Storage

  • Local Storage: All songs and setlists are saved on your device
  • Setlist Sharing: When you export a setlist, the file is created on your device and shared only through the method you choose (AirDrop, Messages, email, etc.). We have no involvement in or access to that transfer

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date above.

Contact Us
